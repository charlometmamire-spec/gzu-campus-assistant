# Great Zimbabwe University Campus Assistant

Flask-based GZU student information chatbot and installable PWA. See
`README-DEPLOY.txt` for the single deployment procedure and `TEST-REPORT.txt`
for verified checks and external-service limitations.


## Official Medical School personnel lookup
Questions such as "dean of medicine" now check the official Simon Mazorodze School of Medical and Health Sciences Dean profile at https://smsmhs.gzu.ac.zw/about/. The handler returns the Dean and published contact details only when the official profile confirms the Dean name. Campus directions and general academic AI routing are unchanged.
