WHAT CHANGED IN THIS CANDIDATE
------------------------------

the app now presents the relevant official extracts and source URLs directly.
It no longer discards retrieved live evidence and immediately falls back to
the older FAQ. If official pages cannot be reached or yield no relevant text,
the existing FAQ fallback remains available. When retrieved fee extracts show
both 60% and 75%, the app flags the discrepancy and directs students to verify
with the GZU Bursar/Accounts Office.

currently reachable. Campus-specific facts must not be generalized between
