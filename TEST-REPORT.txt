## Known external limitations

- AI provider availability, rate limits, and hosting/network restrictions are external.
- Image search depends on configured Tavily quota and external image hosts.
- Official examination timetable files may be temporarily unavailable or return errors. The app should communicate that accurately rather than present unverified schedules.
- The app's PWA shell can cache static assets, but chat and live retrieval are not fully offline.

This report distinguishes static/source checks from live external-service checks; it does not claim that every deployed feature was verified end-to-end.
